Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy covers Daytether: Routines & Care on Android and iOS, provided by Jibin Cherian.

Information stored on your device

Daytether stores the nickname, preferences, profiles, plans and logs you enter. These can include medication and vaccine reminders, appointment dates, selected profile photos, meals, activity and sleep logs. A bedtime setting can record when you reopen the app after bedtime. The app uses these records to show your plan, run local reminders and display your history. Keep private reminder text enabled to limit what appears on the lock screen.

Optional steps and active energy

If you connect Apple Health or Android Health Connect, you can grant read access to step counts and active energy. Daytether displays today's totals and can keep available daily totals from the previous 29 days in local history. It never writes to these health services. These readings stay on the device and are excluded from family updates. You can revoke permission in the health service and continue to log manually.

Storage and protection

Daytether works without an account or sign-in. Your plan is in app-private storage and excluded from normal device backups. On iOS, the plan file also uses Apple device file protection. The app has no developer-run cloud sync, advertising, tracking or analytics. It does not sell your data.

Sharing and export

Sharing is optional. You can preview a family update or choose to export recorded history through your device's share menu. A family update can contain the chosen person's name, routine, timing and status. The selected app and the recipients can keep a copy. Anyone who gets a shared family update link can read the information it carries.

Family profiles

Caregivers can add family profiles, including a child's details, on their devices. Add information about someone else only when you have permission or authority to manage it. The app has no child account registration.

Retention and deletion

Your plan stays on your device until you change or erase it. Open Me › Manage data › Erase local plan to reset local profiles, routines, logs and cached activity totals, and cancel app reminders. For source records in a health service or copies shared with other people, see data deletion instructions.

Website and privacy questions

The website host may process standard connection information to serve and secure these pages. For privacy questions, Open a support request on GitHub. Posts are public. Leave out contact information, photos, health data, family details and sharing links. GitHub processes support posts under its privacy statement. We use submitted issue details to answer you. Use GitHub's controls or ask for help if you need to remove a previous post.
