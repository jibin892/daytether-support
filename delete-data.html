Delete your Daytether data

Daytether stores your plan on your device. There is no online account or cloud copy to delete.

Erase your plan

  1. Open Daytether, then tap Me.
  2. Open Manage data, then choose Erase local plan.
  3. Confirm Erase plan. This resets local profiles, routines, history and cached activity totals, and cancels app reminders.

You can also clear the app's storage in Android settings or select Delete App on iOS. Offloading an iOS app retains its data.

Revoke health permissions

Open the app permissions section in Apple Health or Health Connect and remove Daytether's access to steps and active energy. Manage the original health records directly in that service. Erase the plan as described above to clear cached totals.

Remove exported copies

Delete any exports you saved in other apps or storage. Ask recipients to delete copies you shared with them.

Open a support request on GitHub if you need help. Keep personal information out of public posts.
