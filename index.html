Daytether support

Get help with daily routines, family care reminders and data settings.

Contact support

Open a support request on GitHub. Requests are public. Include your device model, operating system version, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Leave out contact details, health records, family information, photos and sharing links.

Reminders did not ring

Open Me › Reminder settings and review notification and alarm access. Set the reminder to Ring if you want an alarm. Device permissions control exact alarms, sound and lock-screen display.

Activity totals are missing

Open Me › Device activity. Connect Apple Health or Health Connect and allow steps or active energy. Manual logging remains available.

Manage your data

Read the privacy policy or learn how to erase your plan.
